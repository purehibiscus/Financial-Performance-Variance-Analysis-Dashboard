# Financial-Performance-Variance-Analysis-Dashboard

**Dashboard Link:** https://app.powerbi.com/links/Eq06yHsMjy?ctid=f9afa6d3-a503-44b2-91da-b801e2cc43b6&pbi_source=linkShare 

**Overview**

This project delivers an executive-level financial analytics dashboard designed to support budget vs actual analysis, variance diagnostics, and trend monitoring.
The solution enables finance leadership to rapidly identify performance gaps, understand variance drivers, and take timely corrective action.


**Business Objectives**

- Track Actual vs Budget performance at enterprise level
- Identify key variance drivers across accounts, entities, and time
- Monitor month-over-month and rolling trends
- Provide audit-ready, drill-down financial reporting

**Dashboard Structure**

**Page 1 ** – Executive Financial Overview
**Audience:** CFO, Finance Leadership

**Key Business Questions**

- Are we ahead or behind budget?
- Which sections drive financial variance?
- Is performance improving over time?
- Are expenses under control?

**Visual Components**

- KPI Cards:
    - Actual
    - Budget
    - Variance
    - Variance %
	
- **Line Chart:** Actual vs Budget by Month
- **Waterfall Chart:** Variance by Financial Section
- **Financial Matrix:**
    - **Rows:** Statement → Section → Subsection
    - **Columns:** Actual | Budget | Variance | Variance %

**Page 2 – Variance Drivers & Trends**

**Audience: FP&A, Operations Leadership**

**Key Business Questions**

- Which accounts are underperforming?
- Which entities contribute most to unfavorable variance?
- Are trends improving MoM / YoY?
- Where should management intervene?

**Visual Components**

- Bar / Waterfall Chart: Variance by Account
- Column / Waterfall Chart: Variance by Entity
- Line Chart: Rolling 12-Month Actuals (MRR)
- Heatmap / Conditional Matrix: Variance Status by Month

**Key Insights Enabled**

- Rapid identification of cost overruns
- Early detection of revenue shortfalls
- Clear time-based performance trends
- Executive-ready, actionable insights
- Clean audit trail and financial transparency

**Sample Analytical Insights**

- Actuals exceeded budget by +171%, signaling strong revenue performance
- Budget targets exceeded expectations despite flat monthly budget allocation
- Expense and revenue variances reconciled to zero at total level, validating model integrity
- Marketing, COGS, and Operating Expenses identified as primary variance drivers
- UK and Germany identified as top-contributing entities to variance
- Rolling 12-month MRR trend shows growth stabilization around 0.7M

**Tools & Techniques**

- Power BI
- DAX (Variance %, Rolling 12M, Financial KPIs)
- Dimensional financial modeling
- Executive-focused dashboard design
- Variance & trend analysis
